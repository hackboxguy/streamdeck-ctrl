    Drag a button onto any key: an empty key takes it; on a taken key it pushes the keys after it along to the next empty one. Drag keys between slots to move them (onto a taken key they swap). Click a button to put it in the first empty key; drag a key back to the list or press × to remove it. Keyboard: focus a key, ←/→ to swap with its neighbour, Delete to empty it. ◀ ▶ are the deck's own page arrows.
